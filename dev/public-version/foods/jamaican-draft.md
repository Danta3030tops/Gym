## Ingredients used

- **bakingpowder**: Leavening agents, baking powder, double-acting, sodium aluminum sulfate
- **breadcrumbs**: Bread, crumbs, dry, grated, plain
- **brownsugar**: Sugars, brown
- **butter**: Butter, salted
- **butterbeans**: Lima beans, large, mature seeds, canned
- **cabbage**: Cabbage, raw
- **callaloo**: Amaranth leaves, raw
- **carrot**: Carrots, raw
- **cassava**: Cassava, raw
- **coconutmilk**: Nuts, coconut milk, canned (liquid expressed from grated meat and water)
- **condensed**: Milk, canned, condensed, sweetened
- **cornmeal**: Cornmeal, whole-grain, yellow
- **crackers**: Crackers, standard snack-type, regular
- **curry**: Spices, curry powder
- **flour**: Wheat flour, white, all-purpose, enriched, bleached
- **garlic**: Garlic, raw
- **goat**: Game meat, goat, raw
- **groundbeef**: Beef, ground, 80% lean meat / 20% fat, raw
- **ketchup**: Catsup
- **kidneydry**: Beans, kidney, red, mature seeds, raw
- **leg**: Chicken, broilers or fryers, leg, meat and skin, raw
- **mackerel**: Fish, mackerel, Atlantic, raw
- **milk**: Milk, whole, 3.25% milkfat, with added vitamin D
- **molasses**: Molasses
- **oil**: Oil, soybean, salad or cooking
- **okra**: Okra, raw
- **onion**: Onions, raw
- **oxtail**: Beef, composite of trimmed retail cuts, separable lean and fat, trimmed to 1/8" fat, all grades, raw
- **potato**: Potatoes, flesh and skin, raw
- **rice**: Rice, white, long-grain, regular, raw, enriched
- **saltfish**: Fish, cod, Atlantic, dried and salted
- **saltpork**: Pork, cured, salt pork, raw
- **scallion**: Onions, spring or scallions (includes tops and bulb), raw
- **shortening**: Shortening, vegetable, household, composite
- **snapper**: Fish, snapper, mixed species, raw
- **soy**: Soy sauce made from soy and wheat (shoyu)
- **sugar**: Sugars, granulated
- **sweetpepper**: Peppers, sweet, green, raw
- **thigh**: Chicken, broilers or fryers, thigh, meat and skin, raw
- **tomato**: Tomatoes, red, ripe, raw, year round average
- **vinegar**: Vinegar, distilled
- **ackee**: not in USDA: Grace "Ackees in salt water" label, per 100 g, via Open Food Facts (ODbL). Check against a can
